CASE STUDY 01 • CONCEPT & TECHNICAL FEASIBILITY

Investor Status
Dashboard

An in-system dashboard concept designed to improve FATCA/CRS operational visibility, risk prioritisation and management oversight.

Focus
Operational visibility & prioritisation

My contribution
Business case, concept design & requirements

Status
Technical feasibility review

01 • BUSINESS NEED

Reducing reliance on manual extracts and fragmented queue views.

The concept addresses the need for a more consolidated view of FATCA/CRS investor-status queues. The proposed direction is to create dashboard functionality within the existing system rather than relying on a separate Power Query and Power BI reporting layer.

02 • PROPOSED FUTURE STATE

Bring oversight into the system itself.

The concept introduces a dedicated Dashboards area with views for investor status, FATCA/CRS reportability, client health and reporting readiness. Users could select a dashboard and team scope, filter the population and drill from client to fund to underlying investor records where supported.

Current challengeManual extracts and separate queue reviews limit consolidated visibility.
→
ConceptDedicated in-system dashboard views with scoped filters and aggregation.
→
Intended valueClearer prioritisation, oversight, reporting readiness and progress monitoring.

03 • USER JOURNEYS

Designed for analysts and management.

Analyst journey

Open the Investor Status Dashboard, filter to the relevant serviced funds, prioritise Open or Invalid queues and use the view to evidence progress.

Management journey

Open a compliance dashboard, identify higher-priority clients, drill into affected funds and records, and use the information to support remediation prioritisation.

04 • REQUIREMENTS TO VALIDATE

Questions for technical feasibility.

Platform capability

Can the system support dashboard aggregation with drill-down into underlying records?

Filters & scope

Can users save their own client/fund scope while management retains a broader view?

Permissions & data

Can visibility be restricted appropriately, and what refresh frequency and historical trend data are available?

Delivery

What development effort, licensing impact, timeline and administration model would be required?

05 • CURRENT STATUS

Technical feasibility review.

The concept is currently being assessed for technical feasibility. The proposed next stage is to confirm what the platform can support before defining any pilot or implementation approach. No implementation outcome is claimed.
