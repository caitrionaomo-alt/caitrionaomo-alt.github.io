CASE STUDY 01 • PROCESS IMPROVEMENT

Investor Monitoring
Dashboard

A business case and prototype designed to improve visibility of how Hedge and Private Equity investor records reach the FATCA/CRS platform.

Focus
Workflow analysis & monitoring

My contribution
Business case, current/future state & prototype

Status
Escalated for feasibility assessment

01 • THE PROBLEM

Making an operational visibility gap visible.

I identified a visibility gap in the way Hedge and Private Equity investor records reached the FATCA/CRS platform. The opportunity was to create clearer, centralised status monitoring so the team could better understand workload and identify records requiring priority review.

02 • ANALYSIS

From current state to future state.

I developed a business case covering the current-state and future-state approach. The analysis focused on the existing visibility gap and how a more centralised view could support monitoring, workload visibility and prioritisation.

Current stateInvestor records progress toward the FATCA/CRS platform with limited centralised visibility.
→
Identified gapMonitoring status, workload and priority reviews could be made clearer.
→
Future stateA centralised dashboard concept providing improved status visibility.

03 • PROPOSED SOLUTION

A dashboard concept built around visibility.

I created a dashboard prototype as part of the proposal. Its purpose was to centralise status monitoring, improve workload visibility and support priority reviews. The business case and prototype were escalated to management and technology for feasibility assessment.

What this project demonstrates.
