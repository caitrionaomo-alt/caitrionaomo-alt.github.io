CASE STUDY 02 • REQUIREMENTS & PROCESS IMPROVEMENT

Pending Fund Status
& Excel Tracker Retirement

A system-enhancement proposal designed to replace manual tracking with a consistent in-system control for investors awaiting their first trade or close.

Focus
Business rules & functional requirements

My contribution
Problem analysis, proposed rules, workflow & controls

Status
Technical feasibility review

01 • BUSINESS PROBLEM

Retiring a spreadsheet without losing the control it provided.

As Excel tracking logs are retired, the business still needs a reliable way to distinguish investor accounts that have completed onboarding but have not yet completed their initial trade or close into a fund. The proposal moves that tracking requirement into the core system rather than maintaining a separate spreadsheet.

02 • PROPOSED BUSINESS RULE

Introduce a clear Pending state.

PendingOnboarding is complete, but the investor's initial trade/close or share-register inclusion is not yet confirmed.
→
Confirmation eventThe initial trade or close is confirmed and the investor appears on the share register.
→
ActiveThe associated fund investment moves from Pending to Active.

The proposed status applies to both Hedge and Private Equity investors, creating a consistent control across workflows.

03 • FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS

Translate the process need into system behaviour.

Fund-level status

Add Pending as an available Fund Status for an associated investment.

Blotter visibility

Provide a concise Fund Status Summary so users can see active and pending investments without overcrowding the main view.

Filtering & reporting

Allow users to filter by Active, Pending, Inactive or All to identify investors awaiting their first investment.

Multiple-fund handling

Apply filtering at fund level so a Pending investment in one fund does not incorrectly exclude an investor who is Active in another.

04 • CONTROL & EDGE CASE

Protect the status from unintended system updates.

A key requirement is that automated upstream updates must not unintentionally overwrite a Pending status before the required trade-confirmation event occurs. The proposal therefore identifies the need to assess whether a control or override can preserve Pending until the investor's first trade or close is confirmed.

Why this matters: defining the new field alone is not enough. The proposed change also considers how existing integrations could affect the business rule and what control is needed to maintain data integrity.

05 • EXPECTED BUSINESS VALUE

Move monitoring into the system of work.

The proposed enhancement is intended to improve visibility of investors awaiting their first trade or close, remove reliance on separate Excel tracking and TBC trade dates, support consistent Hedge and Private Equity workflows, improve monitoring and reporting, and reduce operational risk associated with manual tracking.

06 • BA CAPABILITIES DEMONSTRATED

What this proposal demonstrates.

Requirements Analysis

Turning an operational need into defined system behaviour and reporting requirements.

Business Rules

Defining when Pending applies, the event that changes the status and the resulting Active state.

Edge-case Analysis

Considering investors associated with multiple funds and avoiding incorrect reporting outcomes.

Systems Thinking

Identifying how an existing integration could interfere with the proposed status and surfacing a control requirement for feasibility review.

07 • CURRENT STATUS

Technical feasibility review.

The enhancement is currently undergoing technical feasibility review. No implementation or realised benefit is claimed at this stage.
